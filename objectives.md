## 🎯 Project Objectives: *Ethical Hacking for Legal Documents*

1. **Simplification & Accessibility**

   * Translate complex legal clauses into **plain, understandable language**.
   * Provide toggles between **original legal text vs simplified explanation**.

2. **Loophole & Risk Detection**

   * Identify **ambiguous, one-sided, or exploitable clauses**.
   * Highlight potential risks like hidden fees, unilateral rights, or waiver of protections.
   * Categorize risks by severity (🟢 safe, 🟡 caution, 🔴 high risk).

3. **Exploit Simulation (Worst-case Scenarios)**

   * Show users **how a clause could be used against them**.
   * Provide real-world impact examples (e.g., losing deposit, losing IP rights, auto-renewals).

4. **Patch Suggestions (Fix the Loophole)**

   * Suggest **alternative fairer wordings** for risky clauses.
   * Encourage **negotiation-ready edits** users can take to landlords, lenders, or vendors.

5. **Fairness & Transparency Scorecard**

   * Provide an overall **“Legal Exploitability Score”** for the contract.
   * Break down by:

     * **Clarity** (vagueness level)
     * **Balance** (favoring one party)
     * **Enforceability** (potentially illegal clauses).

6. **User Q\&A Assistant**

   * Allow users to ask:

     * “Can I break this lease early?”
     * “Does this waive my consumer rights?”
   * AI links answers back to the exact clause for **traceability & trust**.

7. **Compliance Awareness (Bonus)**

   * Flag clauses that **may conflict with local laws/regulations** (e.g., tenant rights, consumer protection).
   * Provide references to trusted sources.

8. **Seamless User Experience**

   * Easy **document upload** (PDF, image scan, text).
   * Color-coded highlights directly on the contract.
   * Dashboard with key obligations, risks, and rights summary.

9. **Privacy & Security**

   * Ensure contracts are processed securely (encryption, anonymization).
   * Offer **on-device/local processing mode** for sensitive docs (hackathon-friendly: mock this).

10. **Hackathon-Winning Differentiator**

* Position as “**Ethical Hacking for Contracts**.”
* Show a real-world ToS (like a social media platform or rental agreement) being “hacked” → highlight loopholes → show suggested patches → reveal fairness score.
